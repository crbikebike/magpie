The team discussed balancing demand with team capacity, arguing that capacity constraints shouldn't halt work—especially in growth where most projects are small tests and hacks. They emphasized that teams shouldn't be territorial about work areas, and that every PM and growth person needs broad knowledge of the customer cycle. Narrow specialization prevents the flexibility needed to respond to demand spikes.

## Topics

- Demand for outcomes regularly exceeds team capacity and shouldn't stop work efforts, particularly in growth.
- Most projects in the growth space are small tests and experiments, not major initiatives.
- Teams should avoid being territorial or precious about specific work areas.
- Every PM and growth person needs comprehensive understanding of the entire customer cycle.
- Narrow specialization reduces organizational flexibility and creates bottlenecks.
